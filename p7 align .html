Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India | Former President of India

About

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played a key role in India's missile and nuclear programs and was widely known as the "Missile Man of India".

Achievements

  • Developed India's first satellite launch vehicle (SLV-III)
  • Key figure in Pokhran-II nuclear tests
  • Recipient of Bharat Ratna
  • Served as 11th President of India (2002–2007)
