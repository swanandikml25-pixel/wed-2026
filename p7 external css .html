Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

"Missile Man of India"

Biography

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played a key role in India's missile and nuclear programs and was known for his humility and dedication to education and youth development.

Achievements

Bharat Ratna

India's highest civilian award

ISRO & DRDO

Major contributions to space and defense

President

11th President of India (2002–2007)

Famous Quote

"Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."
